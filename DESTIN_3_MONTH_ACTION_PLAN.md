# Destin Bangkok – 3-Month Social Media & Meta Ads Recovery Plan

**Prepared for:** Destin Bangkok (Sukhumvit)
**Prepared by:** Social Media & Performance Team
**Period:** Month 1 – Month 3 (next 90 days)
**Purpose:** Honest review of the last period, root causes, and a corrected plan to grow followers, engagement, and sales.

---

## 1. Executive Summary (say this to the client first)

Over the last period we improved the *look* of the account (better reels, images, Stories, Highlights, consistent posting) and confirmed which products people respond to (Andhra Thali and Office Thali Box). What we did **not** achieve is meaningful follower growth or strong sales from Meta Ads.

We take ownership of that. The plan below fixes the three real causes:

1. **Not enough content volume** – the algorithm never had enough reels to test and push.
2. **Ads were built for awareness, not orders** – no offer-led funnel, no retargeting, broad audience, weak tracking.
3. **No conversion path** – followers and ad viewers had no one-tap way to order or book.

The next 3 months are structured as: **Month 1 = Fix the foundation, Month 2 = Scale what works, Month 3 = Convert and prove ROI.** Every week has a number attached, and the client gets a weekly one-page report.

---

## 2. Honest Review: What Worked and What Did Not

### What worked
- Reel and image quality improved noticeably.
- Stories and Highlights now show current offers.
- Account stayed consistently active.
- Andhra Thali and Office Thali Box posts got the best engagement and enquiries.
- Funny videos widened reach beyond the usual food audience.
- Spin-wheel giveaway ad drew interactions.

### What did not work
| Problem | Why it happened | Correction in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Followers did not grow | Posting 2–3 reels/week is too low for Instagram to push a small account. Content mostly menu shots, not "shareable" formats. No collabs, no hashtags/location strategy, no hooks in the first 2 seconds. | 5 reels/week + daily Stories, hook-first scripting, collab posts with food creators, location + hashtag system, giveaways tied to follow + share. |
| Low sales from Meta Ads | Campaigns optimised for reach/engagement. Audience too broad (all Bangkok). No retargeting of people who engaged. Ads did not land on an order/booking action. No tracking of orders back to ads. | Campaign objective changed to Messages (WhatsApp/LINE/Messenger) and Traffic to order links. 3–5 km radius around Sukhumvit + Indian expat interest layer. Retargeting of engagers and video viewers. Offer-led creatives (Office Thali Box lunch deal). Manual tracking of every enquiry. |
| Spin-wheel ad got clicks, not customers | Prize was not linked to a visit. No follow-up. | Prizes redeemable in-store only, with expiry date, plus DM automation to follow up. |
| Engagement stayed flat | Same caption style, no CTA, no replies to comments within hours. | Comment reply SLA of 2 hours, CTA on every post, weekly poll/question Stories. |

## 4. Quality Plan (how we make each piece better)

1. **Hook in first 2 seconds** – every reel opens with the food close-up, sizzle, or a text hook ("Best ₿150 office lunch on Sukhumvit?").
2. **Content pillars (rotate weekly):**
   - Hero products: Andhra Thali, Office Thali Box, Mutton Biryani
   - Behind the kitchen: tempering, biryani dum opening, thali assembly
   - Customer proof: reviews, reactions, table shots (with permission)
   - Offers: lunch deal, group deal, weekend biryani
   - Fun / trend: relatable office-lunch humour, trending audio
3. **Production standard** – natural light or ring light, steam and sizzle shots, 4K vertical, captions burned in, clean audio, brand colour overlay on every thumbnail.
4. **Caption formula** – Hook → what it is → why it is special → CTA ("DM 'THALI' to order", "Tap link to book").
5. **Every post has a CTA** – order via WhatsApp/LINE, book table, save the post, tag a colleague.
6. **Engagement routine** – reply to all comments/DMs within 2 hours during business hours; 15 minutes/day engaging with local food accounts and nearby office pages.
7. **Weekly content review** – top 3 and bottom 3 posts analysed; next week's content leans on what won.

## 5. Meta Ads Restructure (why sales will improve)

### Funnel structure
| Stage | Campaign | Objective | Audience | Creative | Budget share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top | Reach new local diners | Video views / Reels | 3–5 km around restaurant, age 22–45, lunch-time daypart | Best-performing organic reels | 30% |
| Middle | Get enquiries | **Messages** (WhatsApp / LINE / Messenger) | Retarget video viewers (50%+) and page engagers last 30 days + interest layer (Indian cuisine, South Indian, expats) | Office Thali Box lunch offer, Andhra Thali weekend | 45% |
| Bottom | Repeat orders | Messages / Traffic | Past enquirers, customer list (uploaded phone numbers), lookalike 1% | Loyalty offer: "5th thali free", group biryani deal | 25% |

### Rules we will follow
- **One offer per ad.** No generic "visit us" ads.
- **Daypart targeting:** Office Thali Box ads run 10:00–13:00 weekdays; biryani ads 16:00–20:00 and weekends.
- **Creative testing:** 3 new creatives every week; kill anything above target cost per message after 3 days.
- **Tracking:** every enquiry logged in a sheet (date, source, item, converted yes/no). Meta Pixel + Conversions API on the website/order link if available.
- **Frequency cap:** stop ads that exceed frequency 3 to avoid fatigue.
- **Weekly optimisation call** with client (15 minutes).

### Suggested budget (example, adjust with client)
- Month 1: ฿9,000–12,000 (test phase, ฿300–400/day)
- Month 2: ฿15,000–18,000 (scale winners)
- Month 3: ฿18,000–24,000 (scale + retargeting)

## 6. Beyond Meta: Extra Channels That Bring Diners in Bangkok

1. **Google Business Profile** – complete profile, weekly posts, photo uploads, reply to every review. Most "Indian restaurant near me" searches on Sukhumvit come from Google Maps, not Instagram.
2. **Review push** – table QR code asking for a Google review with a small dessert or drink thank-you. Target +20 reviews per month.
3. **LINE Official Account** – Thailand's main messaging app. Broadcast weekly offers to subscribers; collect subscribers via table QR and Stories.
4. **Delivery apps** – optimise Grab / LINE MAN / Foodpanda listing photos and names to match social offers (Office Thali Box as a hero item with a combo price).
5. **Office outreach** – flyer + LINE QR to nearby offices and condos for Office Thali Box bulk orders; a corporate lunch order offer.
6. **Micro-influencer collabs** – 2 per month, invite-for-content basis, collab-post so their audience sees Destin on the feed.
7. **WhatsApp/LINE order link in bio** – one tap to order, so followers actually convert.

## 7. 90-Day Roadmap

### Month 1 – Fix the Foundation
- Week 1: Content audit, new content calendar, 2 batch shoots, set up tracking sheet, Pixel/CAPI, Google Business Profile clean-up, LINE OA set-up, order link in bio.
- Week 2: Launch 5-reels/week cadence, daily Stories, restructured Meta campaigns (messages objective, 3 km radius).
- Week 3: First influencer collab, table QR for reviews + LINE, first creative test round results.
- Week 4: Month review, pick top 3 creatives and top 2 offers to scale.
**Targets:** +300–500 followers, 50+ enquiries, 20+ Google reviews, cost per message baseline set.

### Month 2 – Scale What Works
- Double budget on winning ad sets; introduce retargeting and customer-list audiences.
- Second influencer collab; giveaway tied to follow + tag + in-store redemption.
- Launch corporate Office Thali Box outreach to nearby offices.
- Weekly LINE broadcasts start.
**Targets:** +800–1,000 followers cumulative, 120+ enquiries, 40+ orders attributed to Meta, first repeat-customer offer redeemed.

### Month 3 – Convert and Prove ROI
- Loyalty campaign to past enquirers and customers.
- Weekend biryani and group deal push.
- Build a "Best of" Highlight from top-performing reels.
- Full 90-day report: followers, reach, enquiries, orders, cost per order, revenue attributed.
**Targets:** +1,500–2,000 followers cumulative, 200+ enquiries, 80+ orders attributed, cost per order below agreed threshold, 60+ new Google reviews.
