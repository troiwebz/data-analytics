## Rules learned
- Ask offer economics before brainstorming: free entry vs cheap paid
  entry vs full price + guarantee. Never default the brainstorm to free
  offers — the founder decides the economics per campaign.
- HIDDEN FUNNEL FIRST: always ask what the target funnel first step is
  before designing. Current default: an in-person appointment at the
  client's shop. Offers that don't force that step fail (even nice ones).
- Freebie must be big, physical, concrete ("we come, we shoot") — never a
  watered-down sample.
- Trials are acceptable when short and capped (3 days / 3 deliverables, one
  visit) and when the trial is literally the start of the real package.
- Never spend our own ad money on freebies.
- Max ONE free visit per lead; protect with spots limit + qualification.
- Mix in underused levers (payment terms, effort, exclusivity, ownership) —
  price/guarantee/freebie alone is rejected.
- Liked freebie types: free trial (capped), free result taste (without our
  ad spend), free fix. Every offer must pull into the monthly funnel.
