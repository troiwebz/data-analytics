## Rules learned
- Freebie must be big, physical, concrete ("we come, we shoot") — never a
  watered-down sample.
- Max ONE free deliverable per lead; protect with spots limit + qualification.
- Refund guarantee > free trial, always.
- Mix in underused levers (payment terms, effort, exclusivity, ownership) —
  price/guarantee/freebie alone is rejected.
- Every offer must pull into the monthly funnel; no dead-end offers.
